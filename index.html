About

A practical view of insurance technology.

I’ve spent more than a decade working in insurance technology, from solution architecture and consulting to product and delivery leadership.

I started at Zurich, moved into consulting at Deloitte and EY, led the solutions function at Instanda, and now lead Customer Solutions at Send. My work usually sits between business priorities, product decisions and technical delivery.

I studied Computer Systems Engineering at the University of Birmingham. My final-year research used natural language processing to extract requirements from policy documents. That background still shapes how I assess AI: start with the problem, understand the limits, and be clear about what the technology can actually improve.

How I work

Make the trade-offs visible. Use plain language. Stay close enough to delivery to know whether the plan is holding up.
